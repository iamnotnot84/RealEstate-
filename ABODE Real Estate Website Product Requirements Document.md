# ABODE Real Estate Website: Product Requirements Document

Nov 15, 2026 · @Not Not

## Overview

ABODE is a one-page real-estate website, and this PRD specifies a faithful, animated build of the supplied design so that design, engineering and QA work from one spec.

The design is a single raster page, 1081 × 7954 px (ABODE\_website\_full\_page.pdf). Two supplied images are used exactly as given: the landing-page background (seedream-5.0-pro\_a\_Remove\_all\_the\_texts.jpeg, 2816 × 1584 px) and the footer panorama (a\_Create\_a\_wide\_panora.webp, 2172 × 724 px).

**Problem.** People shopping for a home compare bedrooms, square footage and price, but not how a place feels to live in. The page has to carry the "worth coming home to" idea and move visitors to browse the collection or contact the team.

**Product.** A single scrolling page: a floating navigation plus ten sections (hero, about and principles, "more than a listing" map, featured-property slider, approach timeline, property collection, testimonials, closing call to action, FAQ, footer). Motion is part of the product: smooth anchor scrolling, scroll-triggered reveals, parallax, and slider and accordion transitions.

**Source of truth.** Where the PDF is explicit, the build matches it. Where the PDF is silent or cut off (featured slides 2–5, approach steps 3–5, FAQ answers 4–6, testimonials 2–4), this PRD says what is built and marks it **assumed**, so the owner can replace it with real content.
